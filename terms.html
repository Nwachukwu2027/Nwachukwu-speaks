1. About Nwachukwu Election Watch

Nwachukwu Election Watch is an independent civic platform being developed to support election observation, reporting, documentation and public accountability in Nigeria.

The platform is not the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), a government agency, an election tribunal or an official election result declaration authority.

2. Eligibility and User Responsibility

Users must provide truthful information when interacting with the platform.

  • Do not impersonate another person or organisation.
  • Do not submit knowingly false or misleading information.
  • Do not use the platform to threaten, harass or intimidate others.
  • Do not interfere with lawful election activities.
  • Follow applicable election laws and lawful instructions.

3. Observer Registration

Submitting an observer registration form does not automatically make an individual an accredited election observer.

Registration applications may be subject to review, verification and approval under the organisation's future procedures.

Users must not claim official accreditation or authority merely because they have submitted an application through this platform.

4. Election Results Reporting

Users submitting polling unit figures or other election information must take reasonable steps to ensure that the information is accurate and supported by available evidence.

  • Do not deliberately alter or fabricate vote figures.
  • Do not submit duplicate reports as separate observations.
  • Clearly identify estimates, observations and unverified information.
  • Do not present observer submissions as official INEC results.
Important: Nwachukwu Election Watch does not declare election winners. Official election declarations remain the responsibility of legally authorised electoral authorities.

5. Incident Reporting

Users may submit reports about suspected electoral misconduct, violence, intimidation, vote buying, disruption or other incidents.

A report is an allegation or observation until it has been properly reviewed. The platform does not guarantee that every submitted report will be verified or published.

Users must avoid making knowingly false accusations against individuals or organisations.

6. Safety and Lawful Conduct

Users must not place themselves or others in danger to obtain photographs, recordings or other evidence.

Do not trespass, obstruct election officials, interfere with voting or counting, or engage in unlawful surveillance.

Immediate threats or emergencies should be reported to appropriate emergency or security authorities.

7. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

  • Attempt to hack or disrupt the platform.
  • Upload malware or harmful files.

  • Manipulate submitted reports or monitoring records.
  • Use the platform to promote violence or electoral intimidation.
  • Use the platform for unlawful political deception or fraud.
