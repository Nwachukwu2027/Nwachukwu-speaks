Terms of Service

Rules and responsibilities for using Nwachukwu Election Watch.

Important: This is a draft Terms of Service document. It should be reviewed by a qualified Nigerian legal professional before the platform is officially launched.

1. Acceptance of These Terms

By accessing or using Nwachukwu Election Watch (NEW), you agree to comply with these Terms of Service and applicable laws in Nigeria. If you do not agree, you should discontinue using the platform.

2. About Nwachukwu Election Watch

Nwachukwu Election Watch is an independent civic election monitoring and reporting platform intended to support transparency, accountability and credible electoral processes.

NEW is not the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), a government agency, or an electoral tribunal. It does not exercise official electoral authority or declare election winners.

3. Observer Registration

Users submitting observer applications agree to:

  • Provide accurate and truthful personal information.
  • Use contact details that they are authorised to provide.
  • Keep their registration information updated.
  • Understand that submitting an application does not automatically grant accreditation.
  • Obtain any official authorisation required by applicable electoral rules.

4. Observer Conduct

Anyone using NEW for election observation is expected to:

  • Remain peaceful, impartial and professional.
  • Respect voters, electoral officials, candidates and other observers.
  • Comply with applicable election laws and lawful instructions.
  • Avoid interfering with voting, counting or other electoral activities.
  • Never engage in vote buying, intimidation, threats or violence.
  • Respect the rights and privacy of members of the public.

5. Submission of Reports

Users must ensure that information submitted through the platform is honest and accurate to the best of their knowledge.

  • Do not submit fabricated election reports.
  • Clearly distinguish personal observations from information received from another person.
  • Do not knowingly submit misleading information.
  • Do not alter photographs, videos or documents to misrepresent events.
  • Do not submit reports intended to harass or damage another person's reputation.

NEW may review submitted reports. A submitted allegation is not automatically a verified fact.

6. Election Results and Information

Results entered by observers are submitted for monitoring, documentation and review purposes only.

Observer-submitted figures are not official INEC results and must not be represented as official election declarations. Only legally authorised electoral authorities can make official declarations.

7. Evidence and Media Uploads

Users submitting photographs, videos or other evidence must have the legal right or permission to provide that material.

  • Do not upload unlawfully obtained material.
  • Do not expose sensitive personal information unnecessarily.
  • Do not use evidence uploads to threaten or intimidate people.
  • Do not put yourself or others in danger to collect evidence.

8. Prohibited Activities

Users must not use NEW to:

  • Threaten, harass or intimidate another person.
  • Promote violence or discrimination.
  • Impersonate an electoral official or another individual.
  • Spread information they know to be false.
  • Attempt unauthorised access to the platform or its systems.
  • Upload malicious software or harmful files.
  • Publish private information without lawful authority.
  • Manipulate, disrupt or misuse the reporting system.

9. Review and Moderation

NEW may review reports, applications and other submissions to protect the integrity of the platform.

Where appropriate, the platform may restrict access to, remove or flag content that appears to violate these terms or applicable law. Any review process should be conducted fairly and consistently.

10. Privacy and Personal Information

Personal information submitted through NEW should be handled in accordance with the platform's Privacy Policy and applicable Nigerian data protection laws.

Please review the Privacy Policy before submitting personal information. The current policy is a draft and must be finalised before live data collection begins.

11. Platform Availability

NEW aims to make the platform accessible and reliable. However, uninterrupted service cannot be guaranteed. Maintenance, technical problems, internet disruptions or other circumstances may temporarily affect availability.

12. Limitation of Responsibility

Information published or submitted through NEW may require independent verification. Users should not treat unverified submissions as official electoral decisions.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits any responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable Nigerian law.

13. Third-Party Websites

The platform may contain links to external websites. NEW does not control those websites and is not responsible for their content, security or privacy practices.

14. Changes to These Terms

NEW may update these Terms of Service when necessary. Updated terms will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Continued use of the platform after an update means that users are subject to the revised terms, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
