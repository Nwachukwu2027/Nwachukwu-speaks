About Nwachukwu Election Watch

Promoting transparency, accountability and credible election monitoring through responsible civic participation and independent observation across Nigeria.

Who We Are

Nwachukwu Election Watch (NEW) is an independent civic election monitoring platform designed to support transparency, public accountability and responsible election observation in Nigeria.

The platform provides a digital space where registered observers can submit reports about polling unit activities, election procedures, reported incidents and other relevant observations.

Our objective is to encourage responsible participation in democratic processes and promote access to reliable election-monitoring information.

Nwachukwu Election Watch is not the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), and it does not replace the official responsibilities of electoral authorities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote credible electoral processes through independent observation, responsible reporting, accurate documentation and public awareness.

We aim to provide a structured platform for documenting election-related activities while encouraging peaceful democratic participation.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and accessible election-monitoring platform contributing to a more transparent, accountable and participatory democratic environment in Nigeria.

Our Objectives

  • Encourage responsible and independent election observation.
  • Document polling unit activities and election-related reports.
  • Provide a platform for reporting suspected electoral irregularities and incidents.
  • Promote peaceful participation and respect for democratic rights.
  • Support transparency through proper documentation and verification of submitted information.
  • Improve public awareness of electoral processes and civic responsibilities.

Our Core Values

Transparency

Promoting openness and clear documentation of election-monitoring activities.

Accountability

Encouraging responsible reporting and appropriate review of submitted information.

Integrity

Supporting honest observation, evidence-based reporting and ethical conduct.

Neutrality

Encouraging impartial monitoring without promoting any political party or candidate.

Peaceful Participation

Supporting peaceful democratic participation and respect for the rights of voters.

Credibility

Promoting verification and responsible handling of election-related information.

How We Work

Nwachukwu Election Watch is designed to receive election-monitoring submissions from registered observers.

Submitted information can be reviewed, classified and verified before being presented as a monitoring report.

Observer submissions are not automatically official election results. Official election declarations remain the responsibility of the legally authorized electoral authorities.
