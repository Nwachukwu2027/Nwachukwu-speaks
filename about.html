About Nwachukwu Election Watch

Promoting electoral transparency, civic participation, accountability and public confidence through independent election observation and responsible reporting.

Who We Are

Nwachukwu Election Watch (NEW) is an independent civic election-monitoring platform designed to support transparency, accountability and public participation in Nigeria's electoral process.

The platform is being developed to provide a structured way for election observers and citizens to document polling-unit activities, report election-related incidents and share information that can support credible electoral processes.

Our approach is based on responsible observation, evidence-based reporting, respect for the law and impartiality.

Nwachukwu Election Watch is not the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), does not replace INEC and does not have authority to declare official election results.

Our Mission

To strengthen electoral transparency and accountability in Nigeria by promoting independent observation, responsible incident reporting, civic education and accessible election information.

Our Vision

To contribute to a Nigeria where elections are conducted peacefully, transparently and credibly, and where citizens can participate confidently in democratic processes.

Our Objectives

  • Promote independent and impartial election observation.
  • Encourage accurate documentation of polling-unit activities.
  • Provide a platform for reporting election-related incidents.
  • Promote voter education and civic awareness.
  • Support peaceful participation in democratic processes.
  • Encourage accountability through evidence-based reporting.
  • Make verified election-monitoring information more accessible.
  • Promote respect for electoral laws and democratic institutions.

Our Core Values

Transparency

We believe election information should be documented responsibly and presented clearly.

Integrity

We promote honesty, accuracy and ethical conduct in election observation.

Neutrality

We aim to remain impartial and avoid favouring any political party or candidate.

Accountability

We support responsible reporting and proper review of submitted information.

Peace

We encourage peaceful elections and discourage violence, intimidation and electoral misconduct.

Public Interest

We place democratic participation and the public interest at the centre of our work.

How We Work

1. Observer Registration

Individuals interested in election observation can register through the platform. Registration alone does not constitute official accreditation.

2. Election Observation

Observers document activities at designated locations in accordance with applicable laws and observation guidelines.

3. Incident Documentation

Election-related incidents can be documented with details and supporting evidence where safely available.

4. Information Review

Submitted information should be checked for accuracy, consistency and supporting evidence before it is presented as verified information.

5. Public Information

Verified information may be presented to the public in a responsible manner, while protecting personal information and respecting applicable laws.

Our Commitment to Electoral Integrity

We are committed to promoting credible elections through responsible observation, transparency, public education and respect for democratic institutions.

We do not present unverified allegations as established facts. Observer-submitted figures are not official INEC results and must not be interpreted as official declarations.
