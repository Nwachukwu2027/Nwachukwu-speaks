Privacy Policy

Our commitment to responsible information handling and the protection of personal data.

Platform: Nwachukwu Election Watch (NEW)

Effective date: To be confirmed before publication.

This is a draft privacy policy. It must be reviewed and completed with the platform's actual contact, retention, security and data-processing details before collecting personal information from the public.

1. Introduction

Nwachukwu Election Watch (NEW) recognises the importance of protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when individuals interact with our platform.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services used, the platform may collect the following information:

  • Full name and contact information.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • State, local government area, ward and polling unit.
  • Observer registration information.
  • Election monitoring submissions.
  • Incident descriptions and supporting evidence.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the platform.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate platform purposes, including:

  • Processing observer registration applications.
  • Managing election-monitoring submissions.
  • Reviewing and verifying reported information.
  • Communicating with registered users.
  • Improving platform security and performance.
  • Preventing misuse and unauthorised access.

4. Election Reports and Public Information

Election monitoring reports may contain information about polling unit activities, incidents and submitted observations.

Personal information should not be publicly displayed without an appropriate lawful basis. Reports should be reviewed to reduce unnecessary exposure of personal information.

5. Information Sharing

Personal information will not be shared casually. Where necessary and lawful, information may be disclosed to authorised service providers or competent authorities for legitimate purposes.

Any such sharing should be limited to what is necessary and handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

6. Data Security

We intend to implement appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure.

Access to administrative records should be restricted to authorised personnel.

7. Data Retention

Personal information should only be retained for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.

A formal retention and deletion schedule will be established before the platform becomes operational.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to their personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where legally applicable.
  • Raising concerns about how information is processed.

9. Cookies and Technical Information

The platform may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to support functionality and security. Any non-essential tracking technologies should be disclosed and handled in accordance with applicable law.

10. Third-Party Services

The platform may use third-party technology providers for database hosting, authentication, file storage and website hosting.

Their services should be configured and reviewed to ensure appropriate protection of personal data.

11. Children's Privacy

The platform is not intended to collect children's personal information for election observer registration. Appropriate age and safeguarding requirements will apply.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the platform's services, technology or legal requirements change.

13. Contact and Privacy Enquiries

Questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the official Nwachukwu Election Watch contact channel once published.

Official privacy contact email: To be provided.

14. Applicable Data Protection Law

The platform will operate in accordance with applicable Nigerian data protection laws and regulations, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, where applicable.
