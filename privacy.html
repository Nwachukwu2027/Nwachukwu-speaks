Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 25 November 2026
This policy is a draft and should be reviewed before the website begins collecting real personal information.

Nwachukwu Election Watch (NEW) respects the privacy of visitors, registered observers, election reporters and other individuals who interact with our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information the platform may collect, how such information may be used, how it may be protected and the choices available to individuals.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services activated on the platform, we may collect:

  • Full name and contact information.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • State, Local Government Area, ward and polling unit information.
  • Observer registration and application details.
  • Election observation reports and incident descriptions.
  • Photographs, documents and other evidence voluntarily submitted.
  • Website usage information and technical data, where applicable.

2. How We May Use Information

Information may be used for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Processing observer registration applications.
  • Communicating with registered observers.
  • Receiving and reviewing election observation reports.
  • Documenting reported electoral incidents.
  • Verifying information before any public release.
  • Improving the security and performance of the platform.
  • Responding to enquiries and complaints.

3. Election Reports and Public Information

Election reports submitted by observers may contain sensitive information about events at polling units and other locations.

Nwachukwu Election Watch will establish appropriate review procedures before publishing reports. Personal contact details and other unnecessary identifying information should not be published without a lawful basis.

Important: Observer-submitted figures and incident reports are not automatically verified facts or official election results. Information must be reviewed before being presented as verified.

4. Information Sharing

Personal information will not be treated as public information simply because it was submitted through this platform.

Information may be shared where necessary and lawful, including:

  • With authorised platform personnel who need it for their duties.
  • With service providers supporting the platform, subject to appropriate safeguards.
  • With competent authorities where legally required or necessary for a lawful purpose.
  • With an individual's consent where consent is the appropriate legal basis.

5. Data Security

We intend to use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

These safeguards may include secure authentication, access restrictions, encrypted connections, backups and controlled administrative permissions.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Personal information should only be retained for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

A formal retention and deletion schedule will be established before the platform begins processing real user information.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally appropriate.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Make a complaint to the appropriate data protection authority.

8. Cookies and Website Technology

The platform may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality and understand website usage.

Any analytics or tracking technology will be disclosed and managed in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

The platform may eventually use external services for hosting, authentication, data storage, email communication and website analytics.

Those services may have their own privacy policies and security arrangements. Users should review the relevant policies when such services are activated.

10. Children's Information

The platform is not intended to collect personal information from children for observer registration. We will take appropriate steps to address information submitted by children where applicable law requires it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform develops or when legal and operational requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact Us

For privacy enquiries, requests or complaints, please use the platform's Contact page.

Official privacy contact details will be published when the organisation establishes its designated privacy contact channel.

Platform Development Notice: This website is currently being developed. Its forms and login features are not yet connected to a live database or secure authentication service. Do not submit confidential or sensitive personal information until those services are implemented and the privacy arrangements are finalised.
