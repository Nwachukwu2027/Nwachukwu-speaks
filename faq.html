Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our election monitoring platform, observer registration and reporting procedures.

Important information: Nwachukwu Election Watch is an independent civic monitoring platform. It is not INEC and does not replace official electoral institutions or their legal responsibilities.

General Questions

Nwachukwu Election Watch is an independent civic election-monitoring platform designed to support transparency, accountability, public awareness and responsible election reporting in Nigeria.

No. Nwachukwu Election Watch is not the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC). It is an independent platform and has no authority to replace INEC or declare official election results.

The platform is intended to help document election activities, receive observer reports, record election-related incidents and promote responsible access to verified election-monitoring information.

Observer Registration

Visit the Observers page, complete the registration form and provide the requested information. The registration process will require a secure backend before applications can be received.

No. Registering on this platform does not automatically make anyone an officially accredited election observer. Any required accreditation must be obtained through the appropriate legally authorised process.

The platform is being designed for coverage across Nigeria's 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. Actual observer deployment will depend on operational capacity and applicable legal requirements.

Election Results Reporting

The platform is designed to allow observers to document polling-unit figures and attach supporting result-sheet evidence. Reports will require review and verification.

No. Figures submitted by observers are not official INEC results. Only the legally authorised electoral authorities can make official declarations.

The planned verification process will examine the information submitted, supporting documents, consistency of figures and other available evidence. Reports should not be labelled verified until appropriate checks have been completed.

Incident Reporting

Examples include election violence, suspected vote buying, voter intimidation, ballot box snatching, missing election materials, polling-unit disruption and suspected result manipulation.

The incident reporting form is designed to accept supporting files. Evidence should only be collected safely and lawfully. Never put yourself or another person in danger to obtain photographs or videos.

No. Reports are allegations or observations until reviewed. Information must be checked before it is presented publicly as a verified finding.

Accounts and Privacy

Some features may eventually require an account, particularly observer reporting and administrative functions. Secure account registration is still being developed.

The platform is intended to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws. The privacy policy explains the planned approach. Secure data handling must be implemented before real personal information is collected.

No. The current website forms are frontend demonstrations. Database and secure submission integration are still pending. Information entered into these forms is not being saved or submitted.
