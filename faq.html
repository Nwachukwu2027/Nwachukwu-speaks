General Questions

Nwachukwu Election Watch (NEW) is an independent civic election monitoring platform designed to document election-related observations and encourage transparency and accountability.
The platform aims to support responsible election observation, document polling unit activities, receive incident reports and promote credible democratic participation.
The platform is designed to support monitoring across all 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory, including LGAs, wards and polling units. Actual coverage depends on available observers and verified monitoring activities.
