Meet Our Team

Building a credible network of responsible citizens, election observers and professionals committed to transparency and electoral accountability in Nigeria.

Who We Are

Nwachukwu Election Watch is being developed as an independent civic election monitoring platform. Our proposed structure brings together individuals committed to responsible observation, accurate documentation and peaceful democratic participation.

We aim to build a team that values neutrality, professionalism, integrity and accountability.

Our Proposed Operational Structure

State Coordinators

Coordinate proposed activities and observer networks within their respective states.

LGA Coordinators

Support local monitoring coordination and communication with ward-level teams.

Ward Coordinators

Coordinate information and activities across assigned wards.

Polling Unit Observers

Document lawful observations and report relevant electoral activities.

Research and Verification Team

Review submitted information, check evidence and identify inconsistencies before publication.

Technology and Data Team

Support website development, data management, technical operations and information security.

Organisational Notice: The positions and responsibilities displayed above describe a proposed structure. They do not confirm that these positions have been filled or that the named individuals have been appointed. Official appointments will be published after confirmation.
