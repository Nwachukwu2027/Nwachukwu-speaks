Meet Our Team

A team structure designed to support transparency, accountability and responsible election monitoring across Nigeria.

The People Behind NEW

Nwachukwu Election Watch is built around the participation of responsible individuals committed to credible elections and democratic accountability.

Our proposed structure includes national leadership, administrative management, state coordinators, local government coordinators and election observers.

Our Commitment

  • Political neutrality and impartial reporting.
  • Transparency and accountability.
  • Respect for voters and electoral officials.
  • Responsible handling of information and evidence.
  • Peaceful and lawful election observation.
  • Protection of personal information.
