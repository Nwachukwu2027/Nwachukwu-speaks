Welcome to Nwachukwu Election Watch

Nwachukwu Election Watch (NEW) is an independent election monitoring initiative established to promote credible elections, transparency and accountability in Nigeria.

Our mission is to encourage responsible citizen participation, support independent election observation, document electoral developments and promote public confidence in the democratic process.

Through structured observation and responsible reporting, we aim to contribute to a more transparent and accountable electoral environment.

Our Monitoring Activities

Election Observation

Supporting responsible observation of electoral activities across different locations in Nigeria.

Incident Reporting

Providing a structured channel for documenting reported electoral incidents for review and verification.

Results Documentation

Recording observer-submitted polling unit information for documentation and transparency.

Public Accountability

Promoting civic awareness, transparency and responsible participation in Nigeria's democratic process.

Our Coverage Across Nigeria

Nwachukwu Election Watch aims to develop an observation network covering Nigeria's 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory, including local government areas, wards and polling units.

Important Public Notice

Nwachukwu Election Watch is an independent civic monitoring initiative. It is not the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) and does not replace any legally authorised electoral authority.

Information submitted by observers is subject to review and verification. Observer-submitted figures are not official election results, and the platform does not declare election winners.
