Election Coverage Across Nigeria

Supporting transparent election observation, responsible reporting and public accountability across the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory.

Our Election Monitoring Scope

Nwachukwu Election Watch is designed to support independent civic monitoring of electoral activities across Nigeria. The platform provides a central place for authorised and registered users to submit observations, document incidents and report polling-unit information.

Election coverage will depend on available observers, verified information, operational capacity and applicable legal requirements.

Our Geographic Coverage

The platform is designed to organise monitoring information according to Nigeria's administrative structure:

  • 36 States and the Federal Capital Territory.
  • Local Government Areas.
  • Electoral wards.
  • Polling units.

Actual coverage will be displayed as locations are verified and monitoring operations become active.

How to Participate

  • Apply through the observer registration page.
  • Provide accurate information for review.
  • Obtain any required official accreditation.
  • Submit truthful observations and incident reports.
